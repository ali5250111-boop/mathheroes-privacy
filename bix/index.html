B.I.X

Classic brick play. Modern touch.

Retro LCD gameplay

Move by tapping the left or right side of the playfield, swipe down to drop, and use the blue rotate control. Complete horizontal rows to clear them.

Three difficulty levels

Easy, Normal and Hard pacing for different play styles.

Free to play

Designed as a free game with ads shown at natural breaks.

Three languages

Arabic, English and Spanish can be switched inside the app.

Classic rules

Complete a row to clear it and keep the board alive as speed increases.
