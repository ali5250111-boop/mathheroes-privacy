B.I.X Support

Quick help

Tap the left or right half of the playfield to move the falling piece. Swipe downward to descend; a longer downward swipe performs a direct drop. Use the blue button to rotate. Complete a full horizontal row to clear it.

Settings

You can change language, difficulty, sound volume, haptics, drop shadow, and advertising privacy options from Settings.

Privacy

Read the Privacy & Security Policy.

App issues

If the game behaves unexpectedly, close and reopen the app and ensure you are using the latest App Store version. When reporting an issue, include your iPhone/iPad model, iOS version, B.I.X version, and the steps that reproduce the problem.
